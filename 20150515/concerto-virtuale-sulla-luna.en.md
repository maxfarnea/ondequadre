# VIRTUAL CONCERT ON THE MOON

*Published: 2015-05-15*

**Tags:** concert, album, music

---

This virtual journey to the Moon was born when I discovered "Luna" by **Pavel Klushantsev**. It is a Russian film/documentary from 1965, balancing between propaganda and science fiction. It is a little-known film because it never had an official distribution, as it was abandoned shortly after the American moon landing.

I was greatly fascinated by the utopian and futuristic imagery that characterizes this production, so I decided to edit some fragments and use a selection of my tracks as a soundtrack.

I hope you enjoy the combination of my music with these images.

Have a good journey.
