# VIRTUAL CONCERT ON THE MOON

*Published: 2015-05-15*

**Tag:** concerto, album, musica

---

This virtual journey to the Moon began when I discovered "Luna" by **Pavel Klushantsev**. It is a Russian film/documentary from 1965, poised between propaganda and science fiction. It is a little-known film because it never had an official distribution, having been shelved immediately after the American Moon landing.   
  
I was greatly fascinated by the utopian and futurist imagery that characterises this production, so I decided to edit some fragments of it and use a selection of my tracks as the soundtrack.   
  
I hope you enjoy the pairing of my music with these images.  
  
Have a good journey.
