# PATH FESTIVAL 2014 INSTALLATION

*Published: 2015-05-17*

**Tags:** installation, sounds, experimentation, project, drone, DIY, do it yourself, performance, experimental, DIY, arduino, concert, algorithms, noise, music, electronics, live

---

For the 2014 edition of [Path Festival](http://www.pathfestival.it/), I prepared an interactive installation consisting of a mannequin wearing my optoelectronic mask based on the Atari Punk Console (which I have already discussed [here](../20130309/atari-punk-console.md)), to which I paired a self-generative LED sequencer made with Arduino.

![](../imgs/1836807_10152014996402824_2854117898163133967_o.png)

The light sequencer, placed in front of the mask by one of the mannequin's arms, infinitely produces light patterns whose colors, structure, and speed vary over time according to a stochastic algorithm.

The installation is interactive as it allows direct interference with sound generation by stimulating the optical sensors with an external light source operated by the visitor, such as a portable lamp, strobe LEDs, or the backlit screen of a smartphone.
