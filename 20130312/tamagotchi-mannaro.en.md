# TAMAGOTCHI MANNARO

*Published: 2013-03-12*

**Tag:** suoni, circuit, drone, fai da te, sintetizzatore, do it yourself, sperimentale, circuitbending, glitch, autocostruzione, lofi, DIY, noise, elettronica

---

With this post I want to present one of my best-crafted and most-used drones in a live context: the **Tamagotchi Mannaro**. 

The name comes from a character created by a writer and illustrator friend of mine, [Alberto Corradi](http://ossario.blogspot.it/), who kindly allowed me to reproduce it by engraving it in copper and using it as a contact surface. This instrument was in fact made by combining two circuits I have already presented on this blog: the [[cracklebox.en|Cracklebox]] and the [[atari-punk-console.en|Atari Punk Console]].

  

[![](../imgs/DSCN3689.JPG)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgj57mY_jRxYiNZDz50KWWUMr6tCxUK-HBQFKVPI7v0eRXvPj69qN4CywTtRvUOYJCCTnan6l3enUUvAj6dJU8Vrb8sBTTlxT-ibG6BMIpyydZGr-jdlcKdJAjptuwPYipBvXMcIcF5D74/s1600/DSCN3689.JPG)
  

The Cracklebox contacts are connected to various points on the character's body, while a switch also allows the APC oscillator to be engaged, controllable both via potentiometers and contact points.
