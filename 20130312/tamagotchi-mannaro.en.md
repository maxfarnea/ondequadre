# TAMAGOTCHI MANNARO

*Published: 2013-03-12*

**Tag:** suoni, circuit, drone, fai da te, sintetizzatore, do it yourself, sperimentale, circuitbending, glitch, autocostruzione, lofi, DIY, noise, elettronica

---

With this post I want to present one of my best-crafted and most-used drones in a live context: the **Tamagotchi Mannaro**. 

The name comes from a character created by a writer and illustrator friend of mine, [Alberto Corradi](http://ossario.blogspot.it/), who kindly allowed me to reproduce it by engraving it in copper and using it as a contact surface. This instrument was in fact made by combining two circuits I have already presented on this blog: the [Cracklebox](../20130116/cracklebox.md) and the [Atari Punk Console](../20130309/atari-punk-console.md).

![](../imgs/DSCN3689.JPG)

The Cracklebox contacts are connected to various points on the character's body, while a switch also allows the APC oscillator to be engaged, controllable both via potentiometers and contact points.  

In general I like to add reverb and experiment with other effects as well, perhaps through my Mini Kaoss Pad, making the live performance even more varied and articulated. Here is an example:
